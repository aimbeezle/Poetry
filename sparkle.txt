Boundless dark yet teeming with light
Romance unravels the heart
Giving purpose to those without reason to start

Watching intently with sense, freely observing its oyster
Eyes gleaming to the new, a world of wonder front and center
The way forward never more revealed in view

To see the world now desolate and rough
