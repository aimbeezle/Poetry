Storm pelts the mariner's deck
Each consecutive wave rocks the level
In his thoughts he might revel
Perhaps of simpler times or an incoming wreck
Back when the silhouette of his lover
Crossed the streets of his mind
Without looking both ways
Now she stands still as pine
Looking away, looking numb
Wondering if tomorrow will ever come

He imagines himself at the streetlight
He remembers crossing with her
Tonight he walks alone
Under the moonlight, unsure
