Fly with me, to the horizon line
When sunset greets the sea
Where opposing colors meet to dine
Why suddenly, the apprehension?
What divine intervention?
Was it the place we chose to sit?
How cold was the frozen soul within the pit?
Reaching down from candle light's wick
To frigid wastes where no clocks tick
I knew that when you reached inside
The void would shake your hand, abide
When I reach up to the sky
Past the fiery flames up high
I feel the heat again within
That turns hands to spin and spin
Before our love was horizon thin
But turn to see it sports a grin
