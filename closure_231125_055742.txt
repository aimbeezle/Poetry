She closed the door on me and I didn't even get to see what was inside
We spent long building the door
And choosing the colors to make sure
That at least our eyes wouldn't get too sore
We changed the locks and connected our two rooms
And she shut it, left me alone
When she had the reasons locked inside with her
But I didn't deserve her
Or even the right to atone
I want to peek through the lock and call on the phone
Though I respect her privacy and she told me to leave her alone
