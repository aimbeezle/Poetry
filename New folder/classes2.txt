Kings pray to glory
People pray to gods
Poets pray to story
Each new page another worry
Born of the last hustle
Helping to solve tomorrow's puzzle
With today's words and yesterday's sorrow
Let loose on the page
Fistful part joy, fistful part rage
The pen writes adornment,
And it wrote the holy works in print,
But most important, the torrent of tale
Hijinks, mischief, whimsy, a journey set sail
Of love, of hate, of everything beneath the veil
Between lines, lies meaning entailed
It is a warning as well as a hope
To avail beyond the scope
