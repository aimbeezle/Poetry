I lay disheveled, erroded by thoughts, and killed by the thinker. I stare out the old window, its monumental dimensions decimating the proportions of the wall across from my bed. The sky is clear tonight, illuminated by streetlights. I swear previous skies bore tenacious and shimmering stars, but tonight I see only a canvas painted midnight blue. The subtle shade is uniform, only interrupted by the man-made bulbs to shine upon drivers during the darkest hours. This beautiful yet barren night sky is missing the star of the show. Not even the moon is here to comfort me tonight.
