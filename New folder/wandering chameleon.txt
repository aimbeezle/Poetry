consistent, constant, characteristic calculation
wear the facade amidst your lonely station
pray, give it all away, for elation
or continue the disguise
the exhaustion and the suffocation
live as others intend, not as you will

seeing somebody else in the mirror
the masks piling up the closet
too afraid to clean it out
because of what might be found within
because of what might it be like without
a mask
