for minutes that seemed like hours
my gaze met your face's flowers
time passed its course and it was through
i kept on asking, "what color are you?"
at first, i thought mellow, so, yellow
but your sadness is true
so your color, it must be blue
the worry in your eyes, as well as the fright
your conviction to suffer to make things right
shined brighter to me than a full moon night
