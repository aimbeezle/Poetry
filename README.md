welcome to my repository, i go by the alias: "aim"

this place is a library or collection of poetry all written by me
many of these poems hold some semblance of my mentality, level of sanity, and experience(s) within.
my opera is personal to me in a different way than it may be to the audience.
all my writing is up for personal interpretation, despite my original intentions.
i impart none of my personal beliefs through my poems and any meaning ascertained from them is simply the speculation of the reader(s).
suffice to say:
my work is impartial.
i wish that any audience would experience my work for themselves in a way that makes the most sense for them, hopefully unbound by my beliefs

when art is encountered it becomes new, unique to the encountered.
my experience was during or before the creation.

by sharing, the experience is now yours alone.


to get started, simply click on any of the text files to read them, because it is a collection there are some dedicated to people I knew, or know.
i didn't discriminate on selecting which works to include. whether or not it is embarrassing to have some of these out there, it doesn't matter to me.
embarrassment is often the cost of living a life without regret \~



enjoy
