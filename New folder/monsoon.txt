Freezing winter lingers close behind a bountiful autumn.
Does the tumbling avalanche ever wonder about the humble leaf as it falls from the tree?
They both end up in the same place, rock bottom.
Opposing natures reaching the same fate.
Snow and leaves walk the same path but have never met.
It is much unlike water and oil, separate but always seeing eye to eye.
Sometimes the leaf and I aren't so different; falling with grace and carried by the wind.
But the dominant avalanche stops at no avail, taking all in its path down with it.
The only way to escape its disaster is to have never been there at all.
Yet sometimes, I still find myself treading on snow.
A choice is never presented, only an understanding.
An understanding of the cloudy weather ahead.
