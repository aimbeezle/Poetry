Expression through writing is throwing a boomerang
Writing to let it all go
Every painstaking memory and every joyous moment
It spills onto the paper, with a permanent stain
When you're done you throw it away
Living your life free of one's previous baggage
Yet the canvas can wait
And suddenly, it fatefully returns
To relieve one's grief and relieve one's pain
It'll take many more words to finally regain
Some sense of freedom or some sense of self
To return back to the canvas is almost insane
