Billions of ears will hear not my sadness
Billions of legs would walk past it
But unfortunately, there is one inevitable set
Of ears and legs stuck with me whenever I fret
One set of ears that must listen to my dismay
One set of legs that cannot run away
I have only one mouth that I keep on the shelf
And only one pair of hands to keep to myself
I am the only one who will witness my sorrow
Because the rest of the world told me to keep it for tomorrow
