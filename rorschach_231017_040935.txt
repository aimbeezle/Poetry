it may be the pits under my eyes
canyons eroded low by the sleepless nights
or my boney architecture
standing awkwardly, clumsily, and unfit to fight
perhaps she didnt agree with my conveyor mouth
always having something to say
endlessly cycling the words i sling around
maybe its the bridge of my nose
at the top where my eyes meet sits a terrible mound
i dont know, i never will
i cant tell if she dislikes my evergrowing hair still
it never looks consistent
i may need an assistant
the way that my hair is constantly changing like the seasons
what ever was there to adore about me?
she must have felt bad to have entertained me,
painfully
