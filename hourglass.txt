Succeeding tides erode more rock than their prime
A boulder is but sand to tall waves of time
Mighty mountains stood so monolithic at dawn
Only to rust, crumble below crust to dust by dusk
As love's fading quasar echoes its last influence
My mind is left a spacious void, witness only to your light's pass
These passing memories, erect as sanctuary stone
A remembrance of knots tied unalone
Ticking clocks try better yet to batter my defenses
Yet it's picks and scratches at my reverences
