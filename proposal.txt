Your Only Song

I listened to it, only for the first time
I couldn't understand the words, nor the rhyme
Yet those palpable tones, they spoke to me
That tune, that melody, that voice
Pierced my soul and made me forget what was wrong
It was those same words that filled me up with song
Goosebumps ran through me, my memories
My mind territories, without a place to run
It was you I wanted to marry, you were my only one
