I am well, I hadn't much dwelled on this
A spring in my step and the wind at my feet.
Yet something is amiss, all the while I feel complete.
I despise this feeling, or lack thereof
Did I exhaust just about all of my love?
I could have sworn I had no more emotions to release.
My sanity is wavering when I have no missing piece.
Without the pain and without the suffering I lose my passion.
I want to feel wet in the rain, I want to be low on rations.
Hungering devout poet that might have a few screws loose.
Here is to hoping these emotions don't lead me to the noose.
Hurt me, break me, burn me, so that I may singe my life into this page.

I want all of my work to be filled with unbridled rage.
