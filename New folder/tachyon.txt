On the thirty-second day I was reminded
Of my place, between us, divided
That I broke my nails trying to climb
The walls of this well that were never mine
No specialty, no grace
I've no more masks to save my face
I reach out above with blackened hands
My blood dried out along with the sands

My presence ignored, my impact unswaying
I fought and killed my life by waiting
What insanity I brought so far along
Thinking I was something but never being more wrong
