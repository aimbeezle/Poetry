Want the Gun Pointed at My Skull

I am well, a spring in my step and the wind at my feet.
Something is amiss, all the while I feel complete.
I despise this feeling, being anything but a jigsaw
My sanity is wavering when I have no missing piece.
Without the pain and without the suffering I lose my passion.
I want to feel free I want to be low on rations.
Hungering devout poet that has a screw loose.
Hope these emotions don't lead me into the noose.
Hurt me, break me, burn me, so that I may singe my life into this page.
I want all of my work to be filled with unbridled rage.
