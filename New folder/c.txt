When the vicious waves engulf my ship
Or the breathless deep drowns my captain
I hope the flames of the Phoenix birth me anew
A stronger vessel to sail new seas
A well kept crew without betrayal or thieves
Give me not cannons, so that I may trust
Give me not a flag, to ward off my lust
Sail me out, sail me blue
My exit and return are both soon due
