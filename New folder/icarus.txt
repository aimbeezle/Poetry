A bolstering ego, it festers and swelters
Wilding growth endures like cancer
Show me how to live on the ground
Humble my pride and give me a frown
What is a cure? The warmth, the allure
Dispelled by simple kindness pure
Darkness massed is pierced by heart
By her words spoken I find my fault

I've been among the clouds too long
Watching rain to only fall and never getting wet at all
Believing right, but being wrong
Looking up I found my sin
The water droplets on my skin
