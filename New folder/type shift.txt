the treasure to seek is no longer lone
she stole it in order to furnish her home
my sight lost, i dare not seek
my trap sewn shut, i shall not speak
my ears ring deaf, now forbidden to listen
my legs bound, unable to flee
it is the world which wants to take all of me
i hope the angel only i know will set me free
without my vision and without my words
without my sound and without my glee
i want to be among the birds
embrace me, love, to make me soar
and keep my heart forevermore
