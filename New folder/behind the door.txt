A pure thought: to be remembered
Yet unwise to the last
The truth is often forgotten
In place of a dream
A dream from those risen high
Sold to the deepest trench
If one means to seek to touch stars
They contribute yet again to the cycle whilst on their way up
Bleeding radiance and sweating stardust
Trailblazers with their eyes fixed upwards
Never to look down at the slums of failure below

In this basement lurks an easily forgotten certainty
Its dank walls plastered by our ancestors
Only to be washed away today
Putrid memories of a bygone time permeate the air
Layers of washed out graffiti
Each layer more unrecognizable than the last
What reveling history was scrawled on before
Becomes forgotten droplets in yesterday's rain
