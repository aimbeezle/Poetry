Dashing from scorn and fleeing from scrutiny
The path ahead, lies adorned by moonlight
Shrouded, concealed, the way is walked alone
How lonely is the mind as it treads left and right?
The night air engulfs any traveler
To breathe this air is to seek out freedom
To exhale it is standing in solitude
