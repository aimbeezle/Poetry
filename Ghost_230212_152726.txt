See and can't touch
She smiles and she dances
Her fair skin, dripping
Coveting this contact
The cloud knows not
My desperate prayer to be
As beautiful and suicidal as
The rain drop which graces the lips
I am unable to kiss, the hand
I am unable to hold, the eyes
I am unable to meet, the ears
I am unable to speak, the torso
I am unable to embrace, the tears
I am unable to wipe, again
She dances in the infinity I cannot reach
Her gaze pierces me, it sees into the unknown
