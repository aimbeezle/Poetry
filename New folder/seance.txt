everything important to me; i found spelling your name
amongst every written letter, and every written word
i find a little bit of you in every little bit of the world
and without my eyes to see
without an angel presently
or the voice i prithee to be privy
the devil whispers wickedness to me
at the crack of the whip to the demon untame
once again with this righteous pen; i write your name
