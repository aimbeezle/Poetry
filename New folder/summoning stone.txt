We are the walls around us
The inevitable wear and tear
The sundering cracks of time
Are the only way for me and you
To let the wisdom of the sun shine on through
To illuminate and fill the gaps we had yet to understand
We were dark, once
It was cold in the cell
Now our scars glow
Warm with hell
