welcome to my repository, aptly named after somebody i hold most dear

it is a library, or collection of poetry i've written over many years.
this is technically a comprehensive deep dive into my psyche.
many of these poems hold some semblance of my mentality, level of sanity, and experience(s) within.
the following is a phenomenon born of artistry; my opera is personal to me in a different way than it may be to the audience.
that being said, despite any meaning i gave them, they are all up to interpretation at the discretion of the reader(s).
i impart none of my personal beliefs through my poems and any meaning ascertained from them is simply the speculation of the reader(s).
suffice to say: 
  it is my intention for my poems to remain impartial. none of the aforementioned (truths, beliefs, values), however attached they might seem, should be the main takeaway.
  i wish that any audience would experience my art for themselves in a way that makes the most sense for them, hopefully unbound by my own intentions & agenda.

there's a question to be begged, if it is a deep dive into the psyche of the writer (me), how are your beliefs not inherent to them?
well then that makes one belief i shall hypocritically impart in my answer:
  when art is encountered it becomes new, unique to the encounterer.
  yes i had reasons to jot down every letter, every word.
  everything i was feeling and thinking before i wrote it was mine alone.
  the rest is up to you, however close to complete resonation you might be in thoughts and emotion, it is all yours.

to get started, simply click on any of the text files to read them, because it is a collection there are some dedicated to people I knew, or know.
i didn't discriminate on selecting which works to include, whether or not it is embarrassing to have some of these out there, it doesn't matter to me.
embarrassment is often the cost of living a life without regret ~
