I spent 20 long years hating myself,
before that it was 17,
and previous to that, 14

Hated myself yesterday,
Hoping to wipe that slate clean,
Will I hate myself tomorrow?
Must I live to find out?
Or will my family find sorrow?

Hate is a strong word.
It's use is so genuine and relentless.
I can hate you and I can hate everything, nonetheless
I hate none of you and I hate none of everything. 
I am nothing, and I hate all of myself.

In this hatred there lives passion.
In this passion, exists a life changer.
What I need is hatred of my own fashion.
What I need is hatred of my mind's strife and danger.
