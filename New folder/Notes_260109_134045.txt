I ate nails. I swallowed the iron and rust
Every breath is a conquest
For one lost in purpose, surviving is insanity
Yet I breathe still, reclaiming my life's mundanity
Who knew a nobody would fight so hard?

All for a dream be something
One with only a dream to guard
When nobody with nothing is everything
My labored breaths and my rusty spit

I will be someone, to everyone
