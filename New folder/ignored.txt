the time i will feel the most regret
is when i finally let go of the railing
and plummet past all my sins
all my good deeds punished
by one final regrettable decision
a permanent solution
to what is said to be a temporary problem
yet it does not stop
so i take a step forward
and let the certainty of gravity teach me
just how easy its been the whole time
