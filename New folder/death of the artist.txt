molten potential
molting potential
flaming metamorphosis
your final indulgence
a rage against the shell
thus ejecting extremities
and rejecting amenities
to escape this prison cell
without exception, exists a forge within us all
whether or not we fan the flame and answer the call
we still smell its smoke and sweat from the heat
whether or not we take up the hammer and become elite
we still feel the ringing of the anvil we beat
most forgot they had ever forged even one blade
of which then made; hammer unlaid
of which bell bade a toll for new shade
blacksmiths bleed magma for all the unmade
