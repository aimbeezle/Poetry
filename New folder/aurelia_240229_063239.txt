i adore falling asleep to the anticipation of texts i wont get
and im a fool for believing that every night will be different than before
i forget about the yesterday, i dont fret
its all in the past so i look forward to what i think is a certain desirable future
a future where she talks about more than the superficial things
her feelings, her day, and which fingers wore rings
without the texts and without the overlooked past
the most desirable future is with her at last
because she makes my heart sing
