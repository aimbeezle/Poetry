Her. she is everything. My misfit dreamer
Oh time passes slow, the presence I miss
What can be done about the distance, thread weaver?
I steel my blade to become temptationless
Low and behold, My steel gets duller
On the horizon your shine glistens upon my
Very broken blade. Rejoice! The sun!
Emminently I lay to rest. I hold back my sigh
You sharpen my blade--the fight has just begun
O dearest I wish to be close, no reason to find
Until us lovers, ever so close, with your eyes closed I happen to be blind
