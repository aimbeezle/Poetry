with belly full and shadowed skin
i still yet wallow in manifested sin
as the devil curates my growing chagrin
im left committing sin after sin after sin after sin
to what end does my mind's eye serve?
if it is too clouded by every pinched nerve?
self-sabotage, then
because darkness only comes from the mind of men
as many a man falls; all-engrossed by worldly sorrow
walking into walls and waiting for tomorrow
walking to the well, drinking misery
mistaking poison for cure
when the horse wasn't even thirsty
