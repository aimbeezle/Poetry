Staring at my exasperated figure
I know my soul will not be one to linger
Cross me off the list of dead ringers
Quickly and swiftly I'll get out of dodge
My transient life was a panic-filled codge
With barely any time to discover myself
The door always closed as the clock struck twelve
From time to time I peered through the keyhole 
To my surprise, those flamingo eyes
Staring straight back at me straight into my soul
I fiddled with the handle
My last self-rebellious infraction
But it rusted shut after a life's worth of inaction
At last, doomed of my own accord
I dream of a tranquil lake
As I lay, giving my soul to take
From this dream that I'll soon wake
I know I'll miss the Pink Opaque
