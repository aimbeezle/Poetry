its ironic that with this idea i come to write it down on a page
that ive wandered to this space to put my thought into word
while i showered, i wondered if all i am is my work or my rage
if all my new experiences are just words from my mind, waiting to be poured
my next heartbreak is just another slide on a slideshow, i think.
they laugh and im tearing up my latest woe in the sink
should i keep these all to myself and stop writing, greedily?
or keep delivering on my life story and fulfill the prophecy?
