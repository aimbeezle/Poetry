im a hypocrite
people tell me to practice what i preach
and i tell people that they should be themselves
they shouldnt care about what others think
that they are fine just the way they are
whereas i sit and wallow in shame trying to hide my scar
i have to build myself a garden
i want people to stay and smell the flowers
i will keep on loving people unconditionally
and i will keep on changing for the world to love me
