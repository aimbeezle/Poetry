eclipse

trapped in its silhouette, i follow its movement
the monster roars, my mouth opens because i am its student
why am i slave to the strings? is it systemic?
no, i mean to be more than just a mimic
the beast lurks as i lurk
it chomps when i bite
gulps when i swallow
sleeps when i slumber
it breathes as i breathe
i roar
