I do not dare say your beauty is everlasting
Yet you fit perfectly in the mold the Gods were casting
To be like Aphrodite is a burden
A curse even, something nobody can adore
She was beautiful before
She is beautiful in the present
She will be beautiful tomorrow
You are different, heaven-sent
Your appearance, she borrows
To me it appears our fates are sewn
Aphrodite is predictable, stagnant
You are my temporary fable, 
History didn't deserve you
The future will forget your presence
