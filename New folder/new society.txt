pain, suffering, illness, disease
redundancy on repeat to life's fees; unneeded
disease, illness, suffering, pain
when will these afflictions wane?
for who does not deserve again
to pay the price of the profane?

all of us

thus, we bore ancestral seed
planted in the world of greed
banished in, branded by original sin, man knelt
our leaves floating in the wind, slowly did we wilt
when our roots are to blame, for the shame and guilt
