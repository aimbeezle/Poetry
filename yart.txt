The moon and the sun used to walk in tow
Light used to shine giving the moon a glow
But now it's too harsh it's burning the skin
Come the lunar eclipse, the moon did not show
The sun shined with well-intention to the moon's chagrin
"How do I apologize, oh where do I begin?"
"Say first, that you're sorry for committing that sin"
"Say it again and again for the light you pushed in"
"Give me a day, a day away from your light"
So the lunar eclipse, continued all night
The morning soon came, the sun nowhere in sight
It thought first, before shining too bright
The moon revolved, showing a bright grin
For the moon and sun were together again
