every ear, drowned in bored noise
all too occupied and unable to hear
yet a flower sat on the wayside and lended its ear
a sunflower with its back to the sun
told me id trip if i started to run,
that a smile can replace a frown,
how i could listen if i just slowed down,
and how the ear and the tongue supersede the gun,
i was told by a sunflower with its back to the sun
about harmony, where two tunes make one
and company, where two hearts share fun
no longer was every sound dim
when a sunflower taught me how to swim
