The brightest star that shines above couldn't brave the glimmer that shines out from your eyes.
Volcanos could not spew enough magma to overpower the fiery passion which burns in your soul.
The wind could not knock me off my feet farther than your breath could take me.
Ice could not freeze me any more solid than your grip on my heart.
Waves will never wash me away from your shore.
Nothing could contain my affection for you.
I don't write. My love spills out onto the canvas.
It takes form so you can understand.
