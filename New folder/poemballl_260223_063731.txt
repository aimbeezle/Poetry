it was a pounding pain, at first
from dawn to dusk, a hammering of the anvil
but none of us are blacksmiths
nor was there any blade to temper

the mindless agony of my grief-stricken heart
holding up my emotional facade as atlas does the earth
any tears i harbored served only to boil away by the furnace
then the blackened sky usurped the azure throne
nights grew long and my respite escaped
the sleep i thought i so desperately deserved

doubt remained, better yet; rained
the pitter patter on my shell stole any chance of rest
i still remember the breathlessness of acceptance
and how the combination deconstructed my sanity
i was gasping to reclaim control
grasping for another breath
to fuel the wasted remains of a broken machine
rusted cogs still turning arrythmically at the prospect
of a repaired kind of love
a rejuvenared function
who's form isn't tied to another
who's destination was the bliss of yesterday
