everybody is urging me to be somebody

as without greatness, there is no being

or so i have come to believe
i had not yet understood the depth of what i lacked
a pit devoid of qualities untapped

the first lesson: conviction
my greatest transgression was the absence of mission
believing my aimlessness was fiction
without pursuit, stagnant, unmoving position
discrete efforts make no vain 

the second lesson: arrogance
so belittling, is the title of prince
when peasants walk only the path trodden before them
my path must be my own then, hidden in the trees
mistakenly awakening fake and feigned modesties
such is a humble teapot teem with lies
with aspirations rattling the lid
drowning out any doubtful cries

the final lesson: depression
the happiest beings are the least productive
weathering such a storm requires self-possession
my real self must have died long before my destination
what remains now is machination
churning out what is needed of me
what damning sacrifice, to be, everything everyone wanted to see
like alchemy, to what undoing cost
that: to obtain, something of equal value must be lost
